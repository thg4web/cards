# Plate sources & provenance

Every image used on a card records its source, credit line, and retrieval URL
here. `src-*` files are the originals (git-ignored, re-fetchable from the URL);
`plate-*` files are the processed crops that go on the card.

---

## L 5 · Copernicus (mockup)

### plate-L5-photo.jpg  — front close-up panel
- **From:** `src-lunarorbiter2-copernicus.jpg`
- **Image:** Lunar Orbiter 2, frame 2 (medium-resolution), 24 Nov 1966 — the
  "Picture of the Century" oblique into Copernicus.
- **Credit:** NASA / JPL / USGS
- **Public domain** (US Government work).
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-01 from
  `https://science.nasa.gov/resource/copernicus-from-lunar-orbiter/`
  (asset: `assets.science.nasa.gov/.../lunarorb_copernicus.jpg`)
- **Processing:** trimmed ~70 px of top sky, resized to 1400 px wide, JPEG q82.

### plate-L5-locator.jpg  — front locator panel
- **From:** `src-lroc-wac-copernicus.png`
- **Image:** LROC WAC colour mosaic of Copernicus and its ray system
  ("Absolute Time", LROC featured image 480).
- **Credit:** NASA / GSFC / Arizona State University
- **Public domain** (US Government work).
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-01 from Wikimedia Commons
  `File:Absolute_Time_(LROC480_-_copern_lwac731a).png`
  (`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Absolute%20Time%20(LROC480%20-%20copern%20lwac731a).png`)
- **Processing:** cropped off the burned caption strip, desaturated to ~24 %,
  resized to 700 px wide, JPEG q84.

### src-lroc238-copernicus-context.png  (not used on the card)
- LROC WAC context mosaic — Copernicus (C), Eratosthenes (E), Imbrium rim (I),
  with annotation letters, timeline squares and a 50 km scale bar burned in.
- Credit: NASA / GSFC / ASU. Public domain.
- Wikimedia Commons `File:Copernicus_Crater_and_The_Lunar_Timescale_(LROC238_-_M129418341L)`.
- Kept as a candidate for a wider production locator (annotations would need removing).

### src-lroc473-copernicus-floor.png  (not used on the card)
- LROC NAC mosaic of the Copernicus floor — fractures and a collapse pit in
  impact melt. Credit: NASA / GSFC / ASU. Public domain.
- Wikimedia Commons `File:Copernicus_Seen_Looking_Straight_Down_(LROC473_-_copern_mosaic_crop_subsample)`.
- Too zoomed for a card panel; kept for reference.

## Production note

The mockup plates are stand-ins. The intent for the finished deck is Aaron's
own Seestar / processed captures in the close-up panel wherever one exists,
with LRO / LROC / Lunar Orbiter / Apollo public-domain imagery as the fallback.
